Neighborly Trust, Hand-to-Hand Work

Farm Friends is built on the timeless tradition of local agricultural community support. When harvests are heavy, fences need mending, or livestock requires urgent care, we connect trusted hands with neighbors who need help.

How Farm Friends Works

1. Referral-Based Membership

Trust starts before you even post a job. New members are vetted or invited by existing neighbors, ensuring our directory remains safe, honest, and accountable.

2. Radius-Based Job Alerts

When a job is posted, our system automatically calculates the mileage distance using ZIP codes and instantly emails eligible local hands nearby.

3. Direct Neighbor Support

No middlemen taking cuts. Connect directly over phone or text to arrange fair pay, barter, or mutual farm assistance.

Managing Your Profile & Preferences

Your profile controls how local jobs find you. You can update these settings anytime on your Profile page:

  • Travel Radius (Max Distance): Set how many miles you are willing to travel for farm calls (e.g., 10, 25, 50 miles). You will only receive email alerts for postings within your set radius.
  • Job Alert Toggle: Turn instant email notifications on or off with a single click.
  • Location & Contact: Keep your primary ZIP code updated so our automated distance calculator can accurately calculate mileage for new jobs.

Community Badges & Accountability

Badges highlight experience, reliability, and special contributions within the Farm Friends network:

🤝 Verified Member

Granted upon approval after account verification or member referral.

🚜 Veteran Hand

Recognizes experienced hands with a proven track record of reliable farm work.

⚡ Emergency Helper

Opted in and ready to assist during sudden storm damage, escaped livestock, or urgent repairs.

⭐ Founding Neighbor

Awarded to early members who helped build and grow the local network.

Maintaining Trust (Grievances & Strikes)

To protect local farms and workers, members who violate community agreements, fail to show up without notice, or behave unneighborly may be reported via the Report Issue tab. Admins review reports to issue strikes or remove bad actors from the network.
