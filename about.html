Neighborly Trust, Hand-to-Hand Work

Farm Friends is built on the timeless tradition of local agricultural community support. When harvests are heavy, fences need mending, or livestock requires urgent care, we connect trusted hands with neighbors who need help.

How Farm Friends Works

1. Referral-Based Membership

Trust starts before you even post a job. New members are vetted or invited by existing neighbors, ensuring our directory remains safe, honest, and accountable.

2. Radius-Based Job Alerts

When a job is posted, our system automatically calculates the mileage distance using ZIP codes and instantly emails eligible local hands nearby.

3. Direct Neighbor Support

No middlemen taking cuts. Connect directly over phone or text to arrange fair pay, barter, or mutual farm assistance.

Your Job as Someone Requesting Work

Creating a safe, fair, and clear job post protects both your farm and the workers assisting you. When posting a job request, keep the following responsibilities in mind:

  • Maintain Safe Machinery & Equipment: You are responsible for ensuring all tractors, tools, implements, and site gear are in proper working condition prior to work starting. Regular equipment maintenance prevents avoidable accidents and keeps workers safe on site.
  • Specify Required Gear in Job Postings: Explicitly state in your description if workers need to bring specific personal protective equipment or tools (e.g., work gloves, steel-toed boots, eye protection, or specialized tools). Clear expectations prevent delays and safety risks.
  • Injury & Liability Resolution: Farm Friends operates solely as a community directory to connect local neighbors. In the event of an accidental injury or site dispute, resolution and liability fall entirely on the host farmer and worker to settle directly.

Managing Your Profile & Preferences

Your profile controls how local jobs find you. You can update these settings anytime on your Profile page:

  • Travel Radius (Max Distance): Set how many miles you are willing to travel for farm calls (e.g., 10, 25, 50 miles). You will only receive email alerts for postings within your set radius.
  • Job Alert Toggle: Turn instant email notifications on or off with a single click.
  • Location & Contact: Keep your primary ZIP code updated so our automated distance calculator can accurately calculate mileage for new jobs.

Community Badges & Accountability

Badges highlight experience, reliability, and special contributions within the Farm Friends network:

🚜 Verified Farm

Assigned to established agricultural hosts and farms vetted through community verification.

🤝 Trusted Hand

Recognizes reliable helpers with a proven track record of safe, dependable work.

🌟 Community Pillar

Awarded to outstanding members who continuously support and strengthen the local network.

🔧 Equipment Operator

Qualified to safely operate, maneuver, and maintain heavy farm machinery, tractors, and site equipment.

Maintaining Trust (Grievances & Strikes)

To protect local farms and workers, members who violate community agreements, fail to show up without notice, or behave unneighborly may be reported via the File Grievance tab. Admins review reports to issue strikes or remove bad actors from the network.
